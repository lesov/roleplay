# MASTER TIMELINE — GM / DESIGNER INTERNAL ONLY

> **CLASSIFICATION: INTERNAL.** Every *(real: …)* source tag is **scaffold metadata** for scheduling only. **It must NEVER appear in any player-facing text, asset name, readable save, dialogue, lore book, or item.** Players experience only the Faerûnian event and its consequences, as rumor and news.

## 0. The Hidden Clock

- **Setting:** The Sea of Fallen Stars (Inner Sea) — Faerûn's Mediterranean.
- **Source history:** The Italian Wars / High Renaissance, real years **1494–1544 AD**.
- **Calendar offset:** `DR = AD + 2`. Opens **1496 DR (= 1494 AD)**, closes **1546 DR (= 1544 AD)**.
- **Core principle:** The world advances on this schedule whether or not the player acts. See the spec for the perturbation model.

### Date format
In-world dates use the **Calendar of Harptos** (12 months × 30 days + 5 festivals). Months ↔ seasons:

| Harptos | ≈ | Harptos | ≈ | Harptos | ≈ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hammer | Jan | Tarsakh | Apr | Eleasis | Aug |
| Alturiak | Feb | Mirtul | May | Eleint | Sep |
| Ches | Mar | Kythorn | Jun | Marpenoth | Oct |
| | | Flamerule | Jul | Uktar | Nov |
| | | | | Nightal | Dec |

Festivals fall between months: **Midwinter** (after Hammer), **Greengrass** (after Tarsakh), **Midsummer** (after Flamerule), **Higharvestide** (after Eleint), **the Feast of the Moon** (after Uktar). Days within a month are 1–30. Dates are anchored tightly but most are assigned for rhythm, not derived; move them freely.

### The four braided threads
1. **WESTERN WARS** — Cormyr (the lone aggressor monarchy) vs. a shifting Grand Alliance, fought over Chessenta / the Vilhon Reach.
2. **EASTERN TIDE** — Thay's relentless advance west and across the sea. Its own near-fixed clock.
3. **SCHISM** — a doctrinal civil war inside the Church-State.
4. **NEW WORLD** — the Maztican silver that bankrolls the wars.

### Faction key
Cormyr (Valois France) · Grand Alliance (Habsburg bloc) · Sembia (Venice) · Church-State / Church of Torm at Arrabar (the Papacy) · Dalelands (the Swiss & small states) · Waterdeep / Lords' Alliance (England) · Amn (Castile + New World silver) · Thay (the Ottomans) · Aglarond (Hungary) · Chessenta, prize city **Cimbar** (the Italian city-states / Milan) · Soorenar (Naples) · Mulhorand (Mamluk Egypt) · Pirate Isles (the Barbary corsairs) · Maztica (the New World). *[Cimbar / Soorenar / Church deity & seat are swappable.]*

## 1. PHASE ONE — THE FIRST DESCENT (1496–1509 DR)
*A young monarchy gambles on a southern crown; a coalition forms; the gun changes war.*

**18 Eleint 1496 DR** — **The host musters at Suzail.** Cormyr's young, glory-hungry king proclaims an old Obarskyr right to the southern crown of Soorenar and marches the Purple Dragons and the War Wizards south down the Vilhon road. *(real: Aug–Sep 1494, Charles VIII crosses the Alps)*
- *State:* Cormyr army → Vilhon; Sembia, Church-State, Amn, Chessenta alarmed.

**2 Marpenoth 1496 DR** — **The gates open.** Rather than be sacked, one Chessentan city after another admits the Cormyrean host. The march looks like a triumphal procession. *(real: autumn 1494)*

**24 Uktar 1496 DR** — **A bought passage.** Cormyr betroths a royal cousin to secure the neutrality of a key Reach port, splitting the would-be defenders. *(invented connective)*

**29 Alturiak 1497 DR** — **The southern crown taken.** Cormyr enters Soorenar's capital and installs a puppet under the Obarskyr claim. *(real: Feb 1495, Charles enters Naples)*
- *State:* Soorenar → Cormyr puppet.

**Greengrass 1497 DR** — **The League of the Inner Sea.** Terrified of an over-mighty Cormyr, Sembia, the Church-State, Amn, exiled Cimbar, and Waterdeep secretly league to expel it. *(real: 31 Mar 1495, League of Venice)*
- *State:* Grand Alliance (v1) forms; Cormyr ↔ Alliance hostile.

**6 Flamerule 1497 DR** — **Blood on the retreat road.** The League springs its trap as Cormyr withdraws north; a savage, indecisive battle. Cormyr cuts its way out and keeps the claim alive, but the spell of invincibility is broken. *(real: 6 Jul 1495, Battle of Fornovo)*

**Eleint 1498 DR** — **The puppet falls.** Sembian gold and a Soorenaran uprising topple Cormyr's southern regime; the garrisons wither without supply. *(real: 1496, French Naples collapses)*

**11 Marpenoth 1498 DR** — **Plague in the ports.** A bloody flux runs through the Vilhon harbor cities, killing more than the war did this year. *(invented connective)*

**Nightal 1499 DR** — **Quiet words at Athkatla.** Cormyr's envoys and Amn's Council of Six open secret talks: if the south cannot be held alone, perhaps it can be split. *(real lead-in: Franco-Spanish partition talks)*

**Tarsakh 1500 DR** — **The king is dead.** Cormyr's king dies without a son of his body — a hunting fall, the heralds say. A cousin takes the throne, inherits the Soorenar claim, and adds a fresh one: by old blood, **Cimbar** is his. *(real: 7 Apr 1498, Charles VIII dies → Louis XII)*
- *State:* Cormyr succession (the second conquering king); new casus belli on Cimbar.

**Eleint 1501 DR** — **Cimbar stormed.** The new king brings the War Wizards over the passes and takes the prize city in a season; its duke flees north. *(real: Sep–Oct 1499, Louis XII takes Milan)*
- *State:* Cimbar → Cormyr.

**Higharvestide 1501 DR** — **The duke hires steel.** Cimbar's exiled duke empties his treasury for Dalelands free-companies, swearing to retake the city. *(real: Sforza hires Swiss)*

**3 Tarsakh 1502 DR** — **Betrayed on the field.** The duke's Dalesmen briefly restore him — then, unpaid and unwilling to die for a foreigner, they stand down. He is taken in chains. *(real: Apr 1500, Ludovico Sforza captured when the Swiss refuse to fight)*
- *State:* Cimbar held; Dalelands mercenaries' loyalty = coin, not cause (foreshadow).

**16 Eleasis 1502 DR** — **EASTERN TIDE: the ports fall.** Thayan war-galleys and Red Wizard raiders seize Sembia's trading stations along the Wizards' Reach. Sembia screams for help; the western powers, eyes on Cimbar, do nothing. *(real: Aug 1500, Ottoman–Venetian war, fall of Modon)*
- *State:* Thay ↔ Sembia at war; Sembia loses eastern ports; Thay strength +.

**19 Uktar 1502 DR** — **The secret partition.** Cormyr and Amn sign a buried treaty carving Soorenar between them. *(real: 11 Nov 1500, Treaty of Granada)*
- *State:* secret Cormyr–Amn pact over Soorenar.

**Ches 1503 DR** — **Sembia buys peace in the east.** Bled white, Sembia cedes its lost ports to Thay and turns its full malice back toward its western rivals. *(real: 1503, Ottoman–Venetian peace)*

**Kythorn 1503 DR** — **NEW WORLD: the silver fleet.** The season's Maztican bullion fleet reaches Athkatla — enough to hire whole armies. Amn's southern ambitions suddenly look affordable. *(real: rising New World bullion)*
- *State:* Amn treasury ++.

**Tarsakh 1504 DR** — **Friction in the south.** Cormyrean and Amnian occupiers quarrel over customs, garrisons, and the spoils of Soorenar. Soldiers brawl in the streets of the shared cities. *(real: 1501, Franco-Spanish tension over Naples)*

**Marpenoth 1504 DR** — **SCHISM (first seed).** In a Dalelands market town a hedge-priest preaches that the Church-State has grown fat selling the gods' favor. A small crowd listens. No one of consequence notices. *(invented foreshadow)*

**3 Tarsakh 1505 DR** — **The gun speaks. WESTERN WAR.** The partition collapses into war. Outside Soorenar, Amn's massed **smokepowder handgonnes** and entrenched pikes annihilate a charge of Cormyrean knights — the first time on the Inner Sea the gun masters the field. *(real: 28 Apr 1503, Battle of Cerignola — first battle decided by firearms)*
- *State:* **flag: gunpowder-supremacy era begins**.

**27 Nightal 1505 DR** — **The river crossing.** A winter battle on a flooded river seals the south; the last Cormyrean column is destroyed. Amn takes all Soorenar. *(real: 29 Dec 1503, Battle of the Garigliano)*
- *State:* Soorenar → Amn/Alliance; Cormyr expelled from the south.

**Midwinter 1506 DR** — **A face-saving peace.** Cormyr and Amn sign a truce sealed by a marriage; both need to lick their wounds, and both intend to break it. *(real: 1504–05, Treaty of Blois)*

**Kythorn 1506 DR** — **EASTERN TIDE: a probe repulsed.** Thay tests Aglarond's mountain passes; the war-mages of Velprintalar throw them back, and beg the west — again — for aid that does not come. *(invented eastern beat)*

**14 Tarsakh 1507 DR** — **A rich city revolts.** A great Vilhon trade-city rises against Cormyr's tariffs; the king crosses the mountains in person to crush it and parade through its broken gates. *(real: Apr 1507, Genoa revolt suppressed by Louis XII)*

**Eleint 1507 DR** — **NEW WORLD: blood on the silver shore.** A native uprising against Amnian colonists is drowned in reprisal; the mines reopen within the month. *(invented Maztica beat)*

**Eleasis 1508 DR** — **The blocked coronation.** A northern prince tries to force passage through Sembian territory to be crowned at Arrabar; Sembia bars his road. Humiliated, he begins whispering to Sembia's many enemies. *(real: 1508, Maximilian I blocked by Venice)*

**Feast of the Moon 1508 DR** — **An ill star.** A comet hangs over the Inner Sea for a tenday. Every faith reads it differently; all agree it means something. *(invented portent)*

**Nightal 1509 DR** — **The knives come out for Sembia.** In secret congress, Cormyr, Amn, the Church-State, and the slighted northern prince agree on the unthinkable: to dismember rich, friendless Sembia and split the carcass. *(real: late-1508 Cambrai negotiations)*

## 2. PHASE TWO — THE FIRST GREAT WAR (1510–1518 DR)
*Everyone falls on Sembia; betrayals invert the board; a great king rises.*

**10 Nightal 1510 DR** — **FIRST GREAT WAR: the League of Cambrai.** The compact is sealed and proclaimed: a grand coalition to partition Sembia's mainland empire. *(real: 10 Dec 1508, League of Cambrai)*
- *State:* Grand Alliance (v2) vs Sembia; Sembia isolated.

**14 Mirtul 1511 DR** — **Agnadello.** Cormyr's army catches and annihilates Sembia's field host in an afternoon. *(real: 14 May 1509, Battle of Agnadello)*
- *State:* Sembia army destroyed; Sembia strength −−.

**Kythorn–Flamerule 1511 DR** — **The vultures feed.** Sembia's mainland holdings collapse city by city as each coalition member grabs what it can reach. Only the home cities hold. *(real: summer 1509)*

**Eleint 1511 DR** — **A city rises for Sembia.** One lost city throws out its occupiers and reopens its gates to Sembia — proof the republic is wounded, not dead. *(real: Jul 1509, Venice retakes Padua)*

**4 Marpenoth 1511 DR** — **The siege that holds.** The slighted northern prince besieges that city; Sembian crossbows and ducats hold the walls until the autumn rains scatter him. *(real: Sep–Oct 1509, Siege of Padua)*

**11 Alturiak 1512 DR** — **The Church-State has second thoughts.** Watching Cormyr swell, the high priest of Arrabar quietly absolves Sembia and begins assembling a new coalition — against the very ally he marched with. *(real: Feb 1510, Julius II absolves Venice)*
- *State:* Church-State ↔ Cormyr cooling.

**Flamerule 1512 DR** — **Holy steel for hire.** The Church-State signs the Dalelands free-companies to its banner and blesses a war of "liberation" of the Vilhon from Cormyr. *(real: 1510–11, papal turn)*

**Marpenoth 1513 DR** — **The Holy League.** Proclaimed at Arrabar: the Church-State, a rehabilitated Sembia, exiled Cimbar, and a defecting Amn against Cormyr. The web has fully inverted. *(real: Oct 1511, Holy League)*
- *State:* Grand Alliance reconfigures *against* Cormyr; Sembia rehabilitated.

**Uktar 1513 DR** — **Waterdeep is courted.** The Lords' Alliance, scenting opportunity at Cormyr's expense, agrees to join the Holy League "in defense of the faith." *(real: Nov 1511, England joins)*

**11 Tarsakh 1514 DR** — **Ravenna — a victory that kills.** Cormyr wins a horrific battle, but its brilliant young marshal — the heart of the army, beloved of the king — is cut down in the last charge. The host wins the field and loses its soul. *(real: 11 Apr 1512, Battle of Ravenna; Gaston de Foix slain)*
- *State:* **Cormyr key general dies** (NPC death — design quests around him beforehand).

**Eleasis–Marpenoth 1514 DR** — **Driven from the Reach.** Leaderless and overstretched, Cormyr is pushed out of the Vilhon entirely. With Dalelands pikes at his back, the old duke's heir is restored in Cimbar. *(real: 1512, French expelled; Sforza restored)*
- *State:* Cimbar → restored duke; Dalelands mercenary prestige ++.

**6 Kythorn 1515 DR** — **Novara.** A Cormyrean relief army is shattered by a dawn assault of Dalelands pikemen. The free-companies are now reckoned unbeatable. *(real: 6 Jun 1513, Battle of Novara)*

**16 Eleasis 1515 DR** — **The northern raid.** Waterdeep's Alliance and a northern prince burn across Cormyr's home marches while its army bleeds in the south. *(real: 16 Aug 1513, Battle of the Spurs)*

**9 Eleint 1515 DR** — **Disaster in the north.** A loyal client-kingdom of Cormyr invades to relieve the pressure and is annihilated; its king dies on the field with the flower of his nobility. *(real: 9 Sep 1513, Flodden; James IV slain)*
- *State:* Cormyr's northern client crippled; ally-king dead.

**Midwinter 1516 DR** — **Regroup.** Battered on every front, Cormyr sues for time and licks its wounds through a hard winter. The aging king's health is failing. *(real: 1514 lull)*

**3 Hammer 1517 DR** — **THE GREAT KING.** The old king dies; his bold, brilliant young heir takes the throne and the war with both hands. *(real: 1 Jan 1515, Louis XII dies → Francis I)*
- *State:* Cormyr succession (the third and greatest conquering king).

**13–14 Eleint 1517 DR** — **Marignano: the pike is humbled.** The young king crosses the mountains and, in a two-day bloodbath, breaks the "unbeatable" Dalelands pikes with massed cavalry and cannon, retaking Cimbar. *(real: 13–14 Sep 1515, Battle of Marignano)*
- *State:* Cimbar → Cormyr; **Dalelands mercenary myth broken**.

**23 Hammer 1518 DR** — **A silver crown gathers.** Amn's old council-head dies; a single ruthless heir gathers the Council's reins **and** the Maztican silver into one hand — the seed of the coming superpower. *(real: 23 Jan 1516, Ferdinand dies; Charles inherits Spain)*
- *State:* **flag: rise of the Alliance's unifier** begins.

**13 Eleasis 1518 DR** — **An exhausted peace.** Cormyr keeps Cimbar; the Holy League dissolves into mutual suspicion. *(real: 13 Aug 1516, Treaty of Noyon)*

**Marpenoth 1518 DR** — **Crown and altar.** Cormyr's king and the Church-State strike a concord: the crown names the Vilhon's high clergy, the Church keeps its revenues. Both think they got the better of it. *(real: 1516, Concordat of Bologna)*

## 3. PHASE THREE — SCHISM AND THE SECOND GREAT WAR (1519–1532 DR)
*A heretic splits the faith; one man unites the Alliance; the titans collide; the king is taken, the holy city burns, the eastern tide crests.*

**Feast of the Moon 1519 DR** — **THE GREAT SCHISM.** A renegade priest nails ninety-and-some denunciations to a temple door, naming the Church-State's sale of divine favor a blasphemy. Copies fly along the trade roads within the month. *(real: 31 Oct 1517, the Ninety-Five Theses)*
- *State:* **flag: Schism active**; Reform faction spawns.

**Nightal 1519 DR** — **The princes lean in.** Dalelands lords and a powerful northern prince offer the reformer quiet protection — less from piety than from the temptation of seizing temple lands. *(real: Luther's growing princely support)*

**Mirtul 1520 DR** — **He will not recant.** Summoned to answer for heresy, the reformer refuses, and a Dalelands prince spirits him to safety. The split is now permanent. *(real: 1519–20)*

**Eleasis 1520 DR** — **NEW WORLD: the great landing.** An Amnian captain with a few hundred men lands on the Maztican mainland and marches inland toward a golden empire none of them can yet imagine. *(real: 1519, Cortés lands)*

**Marpenoth 1520 DR** — **The dying.** A plague carried from the True World burns through the Maztican peoples, felling more than any battle. The conquerors call it the gods' verdict. *(real: 1520, smallpox in Mexico)*
- *State:* Maztican native realms strength −−.

**25 Kythorn 1521 DR** — **The superpower is born.** The Alliance's unifier is acclaimed overlord of the whole coalition — Sembia's gold, Amn's silver, the Church-State's blessing, and the Dalelands' levies bound under one head. *(real: 28 Jun 1519, Charles V elected Emperor)*
- *State:* **Grand Alliance consolidated under one ruler.**

**20 Eleint 1521 DR** — **NEW WORLD: the world-circling fleet.** A grand expedition sets sail westward to round the globe and open new Maztican coasts. *(real: 20 Sep 1519, Magellan departs)*
- *State:* **flag: Age of Discovery / Maztica rush.**

**Kythorn 1522 DR** — **The summit that failed.** The great king and the Alliance overlord meet amid impossible splendor to avert war. They part smiling and certain of it. *(real: Jun 1520, Field of the Cloth of Gold)*

**Mirtul 1522 DR** — **Outlawed.** A great council formally condemns the reformer and bans his creed. Instead of dying, the Schism hardens into two churches. *(real: Apr–May 1521, Diet of Worms)*

**Eleasis 1523 DR** — **SECOND GREAT WAR.** Cormyr and the unified Grand Alliance go to war for mastery of the entire Inner Sea. Every minor power is forced to choose. *(real: 1521, Habsburg–Valois war begins)*
- *State:* total war; Cormyr vs Grand Alliance.

**13 Eleasis 1523 DR** — **NEW WORLD: the golden capital falls.** The great native city of the True World is stormed; its temples are stripped, and rivers of silver begin flowing east to fund the war. *(real: 13 Aug 1521, fall of Tenochtitlan)*
- *State:* Amn/Alliance treasury +++; Maztican empire conquered.

**Uktar 1523 DR** — **Cimbar lost again.** Alliance armies retake the prize city from Cormyr. *(real: Nov 1521, Imperial-Spanish take Milan)*
- *State:* Cimbar → Alliance.

**27 Tarsakh 1524 DR** — **Bicocca: the gun rules.** Behind earthworks, Alliance arquebusiers cut down a charging Dalelands pike army before it can close. The age of the pike is over; the age of the gun has come. *(real: 27 Apr 1522, Battle of Bicocca)*
- *State:* **flag: pike obsolete, firearms supreme.**

**Nightal 1524 DR** — **EASTERN TIDE: the island bastion.** After a brutal siege, Thay storms a fortified holy-order island in the Inner Sea, gaining a forward base athwart the sea-lanes. *(real: Dec 1522, Ottoman conquest of Rhodes)*
- *State:* island fortress → Thay; Thay strength +.

**Flamerule 1525 DR** — **EASTERN TIDE: the corsairs awaken.** Thay-funded captains of the Pirate Isles raid Sembian and Alliance shipping with new boldness and new gold. *(invented corsair build-up)*
- *State:* corsair raids unlocked; Inner Sea shipping losses +.

**Eleint 1525 DR** — **The great betrayal.** Cormyr's foremost general — a slighted prince of the blood — defects to the Alliance with his retainers and his knowledge of every Cormyrean plan. *(real: 1523, the Constable of Bourbon's treason)*
- *State:* Cormyr loses top commander to the Alliance (major intrigue/NPC hook).

**Uktar 1526 DR** — **The winter siege.** A reckless Cormyrean push besieges Cimbar through the cold; disease thins both armies and the king refuses to withdraw. *(real: winter 1524–25, French besiege Pavia)*

**24 Alturiak 1527 DR** — **CATASTROPHE: the king is taken.** Beneath Cimbar's walls the Alliance shatters the Cormyrean host in a single dawn, and the great king himself is dragged from the field a prisoner. *(real: 24 Feb 1525, Battle of Pavia; Francis I captured)*
- *State:* **Cormyr king captured**; regency council; Cormyr strength −−; ransom & rescue plots open.

**14 Hammer 1528 DR** — **The captive's bargain.** Held in a gilded cell, the king signs away provinces, claims, and pride for his freedom. *(real: 14 Jan 1526, Treaty of Madrid)*

**Ches 1528 DR** — **Freed, and forsworn.** The moment he crosses his own border the king renounces the treaty as signed under duress. The war is back on. *(real: Mar 1526, Francis released and repudiates)*

**Mirtul 1528 DR** — **The League of Cognac.** A fresh anti-Alliance league forms — Cormyr, the Church-State, Sembia, and the Chessentan cities, all newly terrified of the overlord's dominance. *(real: 22 May 1526, League of Cognac)*
- *State:* Grand Alliance (v3) re-forms against the overlord.

**29 Eleasis 1528 DR** — **EASTERN TIDE: the shield breaks.** On an open plain, Thay annihilates the army of Aglarond in a single afternoon; the kingdom's monarch falls among the dead. The cork is out of the bottle. *(real: 29 Aug 1526, Battle of Mohács; King Louis II slain)*
- *State:* **Aglarond destroyed, monarch dead**; Thay frontier advances toward Thesk and the Dales.

**6 Mirtul 1529 DR** — **THE HOLY CITY BURNS.** Unpaid for months and inflamed by Schismatic preaching, an Alliance mercenary army mutinies and sacks Arrabar. The high priest is besieged in the temple-fortress while the city is put to the torch. The faithful of all Faerûn are stricken. *(real: 6 May 1527, Sack of Rome)*
- *State:* Arrabar sacked; Church-State prestige −−; **flag: the Sack** (radicalizes Reform sects; shames the overlord, who disavows it).

**Eleasis 1529 DR** — **After the fire.** Plague and famine stalk the gutted holy city; refugees choke the Vilhon roads, carrying rage and rumor in equal measure. *(real: plague following the Sack)*

**Eleint 1530 DR** — **The sea changes hands.** Cormyr's greatest admiral — long unpaid and long insulted — takes his war-galleys over to the Alliance, handing the overlord command of the Inner Sea overnight. *(real: Sep 1528, Andrea Doria defects to Charles V)*
- *State:* Alliance navy ++; Cormyr loses its fleet and a key coastal city.

**3 Eleasis 1531 DR** — **The Ladies' Peace.** Two royal women — a queen-mother and the overlord's kinswoman — meet and broker the treaty that ends the war phase. The Grand Alliance is left master of the Vilhon. *(real: 3 Aug 1529, Peace of Cambrai)*
- *State:* western peace; Alliance dominant.

**Eleint–Marpenoth 1531 DR** — **EASTERN TIDE: the high-water mark.** Thay flings its full host against the great eastern gate-city that shields the Dales and Cormyr's flank. The walls hold by a hair as the autumn rains and ruined supply lines force the Red Wizards to recoil. The tide crests — and, for now, ebbs. *(real: Sep–Oct 1529, Siege of Vienna)*
- *State:* **Thay siege repulsed**; Thay overextended; eastern frontier holds (barely).

**24 Alturiak 1532 DR** — **Crowned by the altar.** In a grand rite the reconciled Church-State crowns and anoints the Alliance overlord, sealing his supremacy in the eyes of the faithful. *(real: 24 Feb 1530, Charles V crowned at Bologna)*

**Kythorn 1532 DR** — **A creed, not a heresy.** The Reform churches present a formal confession of their faith. The Schism is now a permanent second confession, no longer a thing that can be burned away. *(real: Jun 1530, Augsburg Confession)*

**Eleasis 1532 DR** — **The republic broken.** A rebellious merchant-republic is starved into surrender; its old banking dynasty is reinstalled as hereditary lords under Alliance protection. *(real: Aug 1530, Siege of Florence; Medici restored)*

## 4. PHASE FOUR — THE LONG RIVALRY AND THE IMPIOUS PACT (1533–1546 DR)
*Peace curdles; a great kingdom breaks from the faith; the unthinkable alliance is bared; the corsairs rule the waves; the board resets.*

**Alturiak 1533 DR** — **SCHISM: the reform league.** The Dalelands lords and a northern prince bind their Reform realms into a defensive league of their own faith, ready to fight the overlord himself if he comes for their souls or their seized temple-lands. *(real: Feb 1531, Schmalkaldic League)*
- *State:* armed Reform bloc; the Schism is now a military fact.

**Kythorn 1534 DR** — **NEW WORLD: the second empire falls.** Far to the south of the first conquest, a handful of Amnian adventurers topple a vast mountain empire, opening a second river of silver. The Alliance grows richer than any power in history. *(real: 1532–33, conquest of the Inca)*
- *State:* Amn/Alliance treasury +++.

**Higharvestide 1535 DR** — **A lean year.** A failed harvest and a savage winter bring bread riots to the Sembian cities and hunger to the Dales. Mercenaries go unpaid; banditry blooms. *(invented connective)*

**Marpenoth 1535 DR** — **A purchased truce.** The great king betroths a child of his house to the overlord's kin, buying a season's quiet he intends to spend rearming. *(invented dynastic)*

**8 Tarsakh 1536 DR** — **THE GREAT SECESSION.** A powerful northern kingdom — Waterdeep and its Lords' Alliance — breaks wholly from the Church-State, seizes its temples and treasuries, and proclaims its own ruler the head of its own faith. A second, deeper crack runs through the religious order of the world. *(real: 1534, Henry VIII's Act of Supremacy / break with Rome)*
- *State:* Lords' Alliance exits Church-State authority; church lands seized; **flag: the Secession.**

**Eleasis 1537 DR** — **EASTERN TIDE: a rare western win.** The overlord leads a great expedition against the corsair coast and storms a key Pirate-Isles port, freeing thousands of captives. For one season the sea feels safe. *(real: 1535, Charles V's Tunis expedition)*
- *State:* one corsair port → Alliance; Thay naval power dented (temporarily).

**Tarsakh 1538 DR** — **WESTERN WAR reopens.** Rearmed, the great king seizes a neutral buffer-duchy on the Alliance frontier, and the long peace shatters. *(real: 1536, France invades Savoy)*

**Kythorn 1538 DR** — **THE IMPIOUS PACT REVEALED.** It comes out — in captured letters, in a too-bold Thayan envoy — that Cormyr has secretly allied with **Thay** against the Grand Alliance. The pious courts of the Inner Sea recoil in horror; some of Cormyr's own vassals waver. *(real: 1536, the Franco-Ottoman alliance)*
- *State:* **flag: Impious Pact** (Cormyr ↔ Thay — massive reputation/diplomacy shock).

**Uktar 1539 DR** — **Proof in the harbor.** A Thayan war-fleet winters openly in a Cormyrean port, its undead oarsmen unloading under the eyes of scandalized merchants. The pact is undeniable now. *(invented connective)*

**Kythorn 1540 DR** — **A truce neither side trusts.** Exhaustion and outrage force a brokered truce in the west, freezing the lines without settling anything. *(real: Jun 1538, Truce of Nice)*

**28 Eleint 1540 DR** — **EASTERN TIDE: the sea is lost.** Off a windward cape, Thay's corsair fleet out of the Pirate Isles smashes the combined Alliance navy. Thay now rules the open waters of the Inner Sea; no coast is safe from raid. *(real: 28 Sep 1538, Battle of Preveza)*
- *State:* Alliance navy −−; **Thay naval supremacy**; coastal-raid events unlocked sea-wide.

**Marpenoth 1541 DR** — **The cost of the corsairs.** Coastal cities pour gold into walls and watchtowers; marine insurance triples; Sembia's economy groans under raids and ransoms. *(invented connective)*

**Flamerule 1542 DR** — **NEW WORLD: a fortune drowned.** Corsairs intercept a silver fleet on the long crossing home. A king's ransom in bullion vanishes beneath the waves, and the Alliance's war-chest tightens at the worst moment. *(invented Maztica + corsair beat)*
- *State:* Alliance treasury −.

**Marpenoth 1543 DR** — **The great expedition wrecked.** The Alliance's grand punitive armada against the corsair coast is caught by an out-of-season storm and ambushed in the surf — a humiliating, fleet-swallowing catastrophe. *(real: Oct 1541, the Algiers disaster)*
- *State:* Alliance strength −; corsair confidence ++; **flag: failed expedition.**

**Flamerule 1544 DR** — **THE LAST WAR.** Cormyr formally reopens the war, and this time Cormyrean and Thayan forces operate openly together — the impious pact made flesh on the battlefield. *(real: Jul 1542, Italian War of 1542 declared)*

## 5. RUNNING NOTES FOR THE GM

- **Reading the dates.** Battles cluster in Tarsakh–Eleint (the campaign season); deaths, treaties, betrayals, and intrigue fall in the cold months. This rhythm is deliberate — preserve it when you add or move events, and players will feel a real martial calendar without ever being told one.
- **Two great wars, as promised:** the First (1510–1518 DR, the dismemberment of Sembia) and the Second (1523–1532 DR, the duel of the titans), with a long cold rivalry after.
- **The Eastern Tide is the metronome.** Thay advances on its own near-fixed clock regardless of the western wars: ports (1502) → island bastion (1524) → Aglarond destroyed (1528) → the great siege crests and ebbs (1531) → naval supremacy (1540). Use it to keep dread building no matter what the players do in the Vilhon.
- **The tentpoles** (protect these): the Schism (1519), the gun's first victory (1505) and the pike's death (1524), the king's capture (1527), the fall of Aglarond (1528), the Sack of Arrabar (1529), the eastern siege (1531), the Secession (1536), the Impious Pact reveal (1538), and the final peace (1546). Design quests to orbit them.
- **Recurring NPCs to seed early** so their fates land: Cormyr's three conquering kings; its doomed marshal (dies 1514); the general who defects (1525); the admiral who switches the sea (1530); the reformer of 1519; the Aglarond monarch who dies at the breaking (1528); the Alliance overlord who unites the bloc (1521) and is crowned (1532).
- **Maztica** is the money-printer and a whole second theater on the same clock: landings, two empire-conquests (1523, 1534), plague, silver fleets — including one a player crew could plausibly raid (1542).
- **Density is a dial.** This list runs 2–4 beats in hot years and 1 in quiet ones. Drop in your own local events (a Dales harvest festival, a Sembian banking scandal, a Chessentan arena championship) freely between these — just don't let a player-driven local event quietly cancel a tentpole; route the consequence into the next scheduled beat instead.
