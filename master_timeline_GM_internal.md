# MASTER TIMELINE — GM / DESIGNER INTERNAL ONLY

> **CLASSIFICATION: INTERNAL.** Everything in the *Historical Source* column and every "(real: …)" note is **scaffold metadata**. It exists to drive scheduling and outcomes. **It must NEVER appear in any player-facing text, asset name, save file the player can read, dialogue, lore book, or item.** Players experience only the *Faerûnian Event* and *World State* columns, and only as in-world rumor, news, and consequence.

---

## 0. The Hidden Clock

- **Setting:** The Sea of Fallen Stars (Inner Sea) and its surrounding nations — Faerûn's Mediterranean.
- **Source history:** The Italian Wars and the High Renaissance, real years **1494–1544 AD**.
- **Calendar offset:** `DR = AD + 2`. Campaign opens **1496 DR (= 1494 AD)** and runs to **1546 DR (= 1544 AD)**.
- **Core principle:** The world advances on this schedule *whether or not the player acts*. Player actions perturb local outcomes; the macro-rhythm (two great wars + a relentless eastern tide + a religious schism + an age of discovery) holds unless the player makes an extraordinary, deliberate intervention (see the companion spec for the perturbation model).

### The four braided storylines
1. **The Western Wars** — Cormyr (the lone ambitious monarchy) vs. a shifting Grand Alliance, fought over the city-states of the Vilhon Reach / Chessenta.
2. **The Eastern Tide** — Thay's relentless expansion west and across the Inner Sea.
3. **The Schism** — a doctrinal civil war inside the dominant church.
4. **The New World** — the overseas rush to Maztica that bankrolls everything.

## 1. PHASE ONE — THE FIRST DESCENT (1496–1509 DR)
*The young monarchy gambles on a southern crown; a coalition forms; the wars begin.*

**1496 DR** *(real: 1494, Charles VIII invades Italy)*
Cormyr's young, glory-hungry king marches a great army south into the Vilhon Reach, reviving an old dynastic claim to the southern crown of Soorenar. Early sweep — cities open their gates rather than fight.
- *State:* Cormyr army → south; Chessentan cities cowed; Sembia/Church/Amn alarmed.

**1497 DR** *(real: 1495, League of Venice; Battle of Fornovo)*
The Inner Sea powers panic and league together. A bloody, indecisive battle on the retreat road; Cormyr extracts its army but keeps the claim alive.
- *State:* First Grand Alliance (v1) forms; Cormyr withdraws; claim retained; relations Cormyr↔Alliance = hostile.

**1500 DR** *(real: 1498, Charles VIII dies → Louis XII)*
The king dies (hunting accident / no heir of his body). The new king inherits the southern claim **and** adds a fresh claim to the prize city, **Cimbar**.
- *State:* Cormyr succession; new casus belli on Cimbar.

**1501–1502 DR** *(real: 1499–1500, conquest of Milan; partition of Naples)*
Cormyr storms and takes Cimbar; deposes its duke. In secret, Cormyr and Amn carve up the southern kingdom between them by treaty.
- *State:* Cimbar → Cormyr; Soorenar partitioned (secret Cormyr–Amn pact); Sembia's eastern trade threatened.

**1502 DR** *(real: 1500, Ottoman–Venetian war)*
Thay's fleets and Red Wizard raiders seize Sembia's eastern trading ports along the Wizards' Reach.
- *State:* Thay ↔ Sembia at war; Sembia loses eastern ports; Thay strength +.

**1505 DR** *(real: 1503, Spain takes Naples; Cerignola — first battle won by firearms)*
Cormyr and Amn fall out over the southern spoils. Amn's coalition smashes Cormyr's southern garrisons in a battle decided by massed **smokepowder handgonnes** — the first time the gun beats the field. Amn takes the whole south.
- *State:* Soorenar → Amn/Alliance; Cormyr expelled from south; **flag: gunpowder-supremacy era begins**.

## 2. PHASE TWO — THE FIRST GREAT WAR (1510–1518 DR)
*Everyone piles on rich Sembia; betrayals reverse the board; a new king triumphs.*

**1510 DR** *(real: 1508, League of Cambrai)*
**FIRST GREAT WAR.** The Church-State, Cormyr, Amn, and the northern powers all secretly league against fat, hated Sembia, agreeing to partition its mainland holdings.
- *State:* Grand Alliance (v2) vs Sembia; Sembia diplomatically isolated.

**1511 DR** *(real: 1509, Battle of Agnadello)*
Cormyr annihilates Sembia's main army. Sembia's mainland empire collapses in weeks; only the home cities hold.
- *State:* Sembia loses mainland; Sembia strength −−; Cormyr ascendant.

**1512–1513 DR** *(real: 1510–11, Pope Julius II flips; Holy League)*
The Church-State, terrified of an over-mighty Cormyr, **flips** and builds a Holy League *against* Cormyr — rescuing the very Sembia it just helped maul, and hiring it back in.
- *State:* Church-State ↔ Cormyr now hostile; Sembia rehabilitated into Holy League; alliance web inverts.

**1514 DR** *(real: 1512, Battle of Ravenna)*
Cormyr wins a savage battle but its brilliant young marshal is killed in the hour of victory. Leaderless, Cormyr's army is then driven out of the Vilhon entirely; Cimbar's old duke is restored; Dalelands pike-mercenaries dominate every battlefield.
- *State:* Cormyr loses key general (NPC death); Cormyr expelled; Cimbar → restored duke; Dalelands mercenary prestige ++.

**1515 DR** *(real: 1513, Novara; the Spurs; Flodden)*
Cormyr is battered on every front. Waterdeep / the Lords' Alliance raids its northern flank; a northern client-kingdom is wrecked in a disaster.
- *State:* Cormyr besieged on multiple fronts; Lords' Alliance enters as opportunist; northern client crippled.

**1517 DR** *(real: 1515, Francis I; Battle of Marignano)*
A bold new king takes Cormyr's throne and immediately crushes the vaunted Dalelands mercenaries in a two-day bloodbath, retaking Cimbar. The First Great War sputters to a close.
- *State:* Cormyr succession (the "great king"); Dalelands mercenary myth broken; Cimbar → Cormyr.

**1518 DR** *(real: 1516, Treaty of Noyon; Charles inherits Spain)*
An exhausted peace. Meanwhile a single ambitious heir consolidates control of Amn's crown and its New World silver — the seed of the coming superpower.
- *State:* Peace (fragile); **flag: rise of the Alliance's unifier** (the "Charles V" figure) begins.

## 3. PHASE THREE — SCHISM AND THE SECOND GREAT WAR (1519–1532 DR)
*A heretic splits the church; one figure unites the Alliance; the titans collide; the king is taken and the holy city burns; the eastern tide crests.*

**1519 DR** *(real: 1517, the Reformation)*
**THE GREAT SCHISM.** A renegade priest nails his denunciations to the temple doors. Doctrine splits; reform sects spread fast through the Dalelands, whose princes seize temple lands and revenues.
- *State:* **flag: Schism active**; Church-State revenue −; Dalelands princes gain temple wealth; new "Reform" faction spawns.

**1521 DR** *(real: 1519, Charles V elected Emperor; Magellan sails)*
The Alliance's unifier is acclaimed overlord of the whole coalition — now commanding Sembia's gold, Amn's silver, the Church-State's blessing, and the Dalelands' levies as one bloc. A grand expedition sets sail to circle the world / open new Maztican coasts.
- *State:* **Grand Alliance consolidated under one head** (superpower online); **flag: Age of Discovery / Maztica rush**; Amn colonial income ++.

**1523 DR** *(real: 1521, Habsburg–Valois war begins)*
**SECOND GREAT WAR.** Cormyr and the now-unified Grand Alliance go to war for mastery of the entire Inner Sea.
- *State:* Total war Cormyr vs Grand Alliance; all minor powers forced to choose sides.

**1524 DR** *(real: 1522, Bicocca; Ottomans take Rhodes)*
Alliance smokepowder columns shatter a Dalelands pike army utterly — the pike age is over, the gun rules. Cimbar falls to the Alliance again. Far east, **Thay storms a fortified holy-order island** in the Inner Sea.
- *State:* Cimbar → Alliance; **flag: pike obsolete, firearms dominant**; Pirate Isles/holy-order fortress → Thay; Thay strength +.

**1527 DR** *(real: 1525, Battle of Pavia — Francis I captured)*
**CATASTROPHE.** Cormyr's great king is defeated and **taken prisoner** in a disastrous pitched battle. Cormyr plunges into regency, ransom panic, and faction infighting.
- *State:* **Cormyr king captured** (major event); regency council; Cormyr strength −−; ransom plot hooks.

**1528 DR** *(real: 1526, Treaty of Madrid repudiated; League of Cognac; **Battle of Mohács**)*
The king is freed on humiliating terms he immediately renounces; a fresh anti-Alliance league forms (Cormyr + Church-State + Sembia + the city-states). **In the same year, Thay annihilates Aglarond** at a field battle that kills its monarch. The eastern shield is gone; Thay pours west.
- *State:* King freed, treaty void; new league (v3) vs Alliance; **Aglarond destroyed, monarch dead**; Thay border advances to the Dalelands' doorstep.

**1529 DR** *(real: 1527, **Sack of Rome**)*
**THE HOLY CITY BURNS.** Unpaid, schism-radicalized Alliance mercenaries mutiny and sack **Arrabar**, the Church-State's seat. The high priest is besieged in the temple-fortress for months. The faithful across Faerûn are traumatized; the Alliance's leader is blamed even as he disavows it.
- *State:* Arrabar sacked; Church-State prestige −−; **flag: Sack** (radicalizes Reform sects, shames the Alliance); Church-State ↔ Alliance strained.

**1531 DR** *(real: 1529, Ladies' Peace at Cambrai; **Siege of Vienna**)*
Two royal women broker the peace that ends the war phase; the Grand Alliance is left dominant over the Vilhon. **At the same hour, Thay lays siege to the great gate-city of the east** (the frontier bastion shielding the Dalelands/Cormyr from the steppe) and is barely, bloodily repulsed. High-water mark of the Eastern Tide.
- *State:* Western peace (Alliance dominant); **Thay siege repulsed** (eastern frontier holds — just); Thay overextended.

**1532 DR** *(real: 1530, imperial coronation; Medici restored in Florence)*
The Alliance's overlord is formally crowned and anointed by the (grudgingly reconciled) Church-State. A rebellious merchant-republic city is reduced and its old banking dynasty reinstalled as hereditary lords.
- *State:* Alliance leader legitimized/crowned; one republic → restored banking dynasty; Church-State ↔ Alliance patched.

## 4. PHASE FOUR — THE LONG RIVALRY AND THE IMPIOUS PACT (1537–1546 DR)
*Peace curdles; the unthinkable alliance is revealed; the corsairs rule the waves; the campaign closes with the board reset for the next generation.*

**1537–1540 DR** *(real: 1535–38, renewed war; **Franco-Ottoman alliance 1536**; Truce of Nice)*
Cormyr reopens the war by seizing a neutral buffer-duchy on the Alliance frontier. Then the unthinkable surfaces: **Cormyr has made a secret pact with Thay** against the Grand Alliance. Scandal rocks every court; pious nations recoil. A brokered truce freezes the lines.
- *State:* Buffer-duchy → Cormyr; **flag: Impious Pact revealed** (Cormyr ↔ Thay covert alliance — huge reputation/diplomacy shock); truce.

**1540 DR** *(real: 1538, Battle of Preveza)*
Thay's corsair fleet out of the Pirate Isles smashes the combined Alliance navy. Thay now rules the open waters of the Inner Sea; coastal trade and cities live under threat of raid.
- *State:* Alliance navy −−; **Thay naval supremacy on the Inner Sea**; coastal raid events unlocked.

**1543 DR** *(real: 1541, the Algiers disaster)*
The Alliance's grand punitive expedition against the corsair coast is wrecked by storm and ambush — a humiliating, fleet-swallowing catastrophe.
- *State:* Alliance strength −; corsair confidence ++; **flag: failed expedition**.

**1544–1546 DR** *(real: 1542–44, final Italian War; Ceresole; Crépy)*
**THE LAST WAR OF THE CAMPAIGN.** Open Cormyr–Thay joint operations: a combined fleet besieges a coastal city while Cormyr wins a hard field victory in the buffer-duchy. In answer, the Lords' Alliance and the Grand Alliance invade Cormyr from the north. Exhaustion forces a comprehensive peace at the campaign's end — every border redrawn, every grudge banked.
- *State:* Combined Cormyr/Thay offensive; Lords' Alliance + Grand Alliance counter-invasion of Cormyr; **comprehensive peace (1546 DR)**; *board reset for the next 50-year cycle.*

## 5. RUNNING NOTES FOR THE GM

- **Two great wars, as promised:** the First Great War (1510–1518 DR) and the Second Great War (1523–1532 DR), with a long cold rivalry after.
- **The Eastern Tide is the metronome:** Thay advances on a near-fixed schedule (1502 ports → 1524 island → 1528 Aglarond → 1531 great siege → 1540 naval supremacy). It does not care who wins in the west. Use it to keep dread building no matter what the players do in the Vilhon.
- **Pre-loaded detonations:** the Schism (1519), the king's capture (1527), the Sack of Arrabar (1529), and the Impious Pact reveal (1537–40). These are your tentpoles — design quests to orbit them.
- **Swappable names** are marked *[swappable]*: the Church-State's deity and seat, the prize city, and the southern crown. Lock these before the coding agent ingests the spec, or leave the IDs and rename labels later.
- **The New World** (Maztica) is your pressure-release valve and money-printer: overseas silver explains how the Alliance can afford endless war, and gives players an entire second theater that runs on the same hidden clock (conquest, plague, gold fleets).
